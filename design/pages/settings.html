Slack and Telegram bridges

A bridge mirrors one Harness channel with one Slack channel or Telegram chat, both ways. Only people who linked their own account can post from Slack or Telegram. Messages from anyone else are ignored. The Slack and Telegram tokens are set on the instance, not here.

#releases is bridged to SlackC04F9K2LM
Harness channel
#releases ▾
Service
Slack ▾
Slack channel id (starts with C)
Link your own account
1 Bridge a channel
2 Get your link code
3 Send it to the Harness app
In a private Slack message to the Harness app, send:
/link 7QK2XM9P
The code works once and expires at 14:20 (10 minutes). After that, your messages in the bridged channel appear in Harness under your name.
Telegram link codes are under Account, in Where notifications go.
