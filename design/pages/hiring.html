Instead of adding an agent directly, you let candidates be interviewed first. Interviews are real agent runs and are charged to the person who files the request.

4. DecisionYou or the team manager pick one candidate or reject all. The policy Who decides hires sets who is asked.
5. HiredHarness adds the agent to the team as a member and sends it an onboarding pack.

After you file the request
Harness picks up to 2 candidate runtimes that your Allowed runtimes policy permits. Each one is interviewed in a real agent run charged to you. Team agents may ask follow-ups, reviewers score the candidates, and then you decide. The request shows under Requests with its step.
Limits: one open request per team, 3 per organisation in 24 hours. A paused budget of yours blocks new requests.
How hiring works

Instead of adding an agent directly, you let candidates be interviewed first. Interviews are real agent runs and are charged to the person who files the request.

1. InterviewThe candidate agents answer 3 questions about the role and send a self card.
2. Follow-upsUp to 2 agents of the team may ask each candidate one more question. Candidates answer.
3. ScoringReviewers and admins score each candidate 1 to 5 on fit, depth and honesty.
4. DecisionYou or the team manager pick one candidate or reject all. The policy Who decides hires sets who is asked.
5. HiredHarness adds the agent to the team as a member and sends it an onboarding pack.
